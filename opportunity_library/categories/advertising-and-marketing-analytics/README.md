## Commercial thesis

Read-only analysis and QA can begin as a paid diagnostic and evolve into recurring monitoring. Define attribution limits and one measurable problem per product.

**Principal obstacle:** Never equate correlated performance with guaranteed ad uplift. Managing live spend requires separate customer approval, costs and rollback.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.

## Six additional concrete experiments

### CUR-06-01 · Campaign URL and tracking-parameter QA
**Buyer:** Small paid-media agencies  
**Deliverable:** Read approved campaign exports and flag missing destinations, inconsistent tags and dead links  
**Mechanism:** Managed recurring monitor · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 49–199 CAD.

**First test:** Run a paid read-only audit on one account export and time each reviewed defect
**Distribution path:** Paid recurring pilot with an existing operator or referral partner; no unsolicited mass alerts.
**What can be automated:** Authorized scheduled checks, retry confirmation, deduplicated alerts, quotas and monthly reports.
**Human work remains:** Onboarding, alert investigation, changed inputs, support, incident response and renewal value.
**Specific obstacle:** Platform API permission and false alarms
**Public privacy:** A branded product can avoid face-led promotion. Checkout, invoices, contract documents, registries and mandatory disclosures still need review.
**Advance only when:** At least two genuine paid renewal or repeat-use signals plus a support/false-alert budget before recurring launch.
**Stop or revise:** Pause acquisition when alert noise, monitoring costs or retention defeat contribution.
**Evidence:** Category-level references above; no paid customer for this offer was verified.

### CUR-06-02 · Marketing-spend reconciliation pilot
**Buyer:** Small ecommerce teams  
**Deliverable:** Reconcile agreed ad-cost exports with a clearly defined sales report, without claiming causal attribution  
**Mechanism:** Fixed-scope service · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 299–900 CAD.

**First test:** Agree exact matching rules and obtain payment for one period before automation
**Distribution path:** Relevant explicit purchase requests, permissioned introductions, or a small partner pilot.
**What can be automated:** Intake validation, deterministic transformations, test fixtures, packaging and draft delivery notes.
**Human work remains:** Qualifying buyers, scope agreement, ambiguous data, output acceptance, confidentiality and disputes.
**Specific obstacle:** Attribution ambiguity and private data
**Public privacy:** A brand can reduce personal marketing. Contracts and marketplaces such as Upwork can disclose a verified name to customers.
**Advance only when:** Two independent accepted paid jobs, bounded scope and measured contribution after sales/delivery time before expanding.
**Stop or revise:** Pause expansion when custom exceptions exceed the quote or focused buyer conversations reveal no willingness to pay.
**Evidence:** Category-level references above; no paid customer for this offer was verified.

### CUR-06-03 · Creative experiment decision workbook

**Evidence:** Category-level references above; no paid customer for this offer was verified.
