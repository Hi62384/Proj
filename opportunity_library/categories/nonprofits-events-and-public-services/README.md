## Commercial thesis

Reusable coordination, reporting and information tools can be brand-led. Sell to an authorized buyer and keep permissions, accessibility and records clear.

**Principal obstacle:** Charitable receipting, public procurement, children’s data, election operations and funding eligibility need specific review; no automated official decisions.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.

**Buyer:** Small nonprofits  
**Deliverable:** Organize authorized milestones, expenses and evidence links without deciding eligibility  
**Mechanism:** Self-service digital download · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 39–149 CAD.

**First test:** Sell a reviewed administrative template for one reporting format
**Distribution path:** Permitted original-product marketplace, useful sample, opt-in audience or a specialist distribution partner.
**What can be automated:** Reproducible builds, compatibility tests, versioned downloads, licence records and documented onboarding.
