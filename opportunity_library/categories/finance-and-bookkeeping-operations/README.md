## Commercial thesis

Keep initial products administrative: reconcile agreed exports, organize records and calculate explicitly defined scenarios. Leave filing, advice and regulated conclusions to qualified people.

**Principal obstacle:** Financial errors cause harm. Private banking records, tax scope and jurisdiction-specific rules make expert review and accurate disclaimers necessary.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.
