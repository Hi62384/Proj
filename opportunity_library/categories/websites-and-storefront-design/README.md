## Commercial thesis

Reusable tested components can be sold independently of bespoke site-building. Target one concrete integration or design gap and ship setup documentation.

**Principal obstacle:** A template marketplace is supply evidence, not buyers for your item. Security, accessibility, licensing and platform version support add work.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.

## Six additional concrete experiments

### CUR-14-01 · Original local-service website template
**Buyer:** Small web agencies  
**Deliverable:** A responsive editable site template with service pages, original assets and accessibility notes  
**Mechanism:** Original licensed asset · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 49–199 CAD.

**First test:** Sell one tested template via an approved marketplace or direct demo
**Distribution path:** Eligible original-asset marketplace, honest previews and a useful sampler with clear commercial rights.
**What can be automated:** File/format QA, original asset variant export, previews, packaging, licensing and digital fulfillment.
**Human work remains:** Art direction, authorship/rights, compatibility, product usefulness, promotion and customer support.
**Specific obstacle:** Market saturation and platform compatibility
**Public privacy:** A branded product can avoid face-led promotion. Checkout, invoices, contract documents, registries and mandatory disclosures still need review.
**Advance only when:** Buyers prefer the sample to relevant free/paid alternatives and at least two pay for a finished useful pack.
**Stop or revise:** Stop expanding a pack that fails a craft/time test or has no paid adoption after qualified demonstrations.
**Evidence:** Category-level references above; no paid customer for this offer was verified.

### CUR-14-06 · Owner-authorized release smoke checks
**Buyer:** Small web agencies  
**Deliverable:** Repeat a few approved noncritical browser tests after site deployments  
**Mechanism:** Managed recurring monitor · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 49–199 CAD.

**First test:** Sell a capped read-only/test-account pilot with exact response terms
**Distribution path:** Paid recurring pilot with an existing operator or referral partner; no unsolicited mass alerts.
**What can be automated:** Authorized scheduled checks, retry confirmation, deduplicated alerts, quotas and monthly reports.
**Human work remains:** Onboarding, alert investigation, changed inputs, support, incident response and renewal value.
**Specific obstacle:** False alerts and production side effects
**Public privacy:** A branded product can avoid face-led promotion. Checkout, invoices, contract documents, registries and mandatory disclosures still need review.
**Advance only when:** At least two genuine paid renewal or repeat-use signals plus a support/false-alert budget before recurring launch.
**Stop or revise:** Pause acquisition when alert noise, monitoring costs or retention defeat contribution.
**Evidence:** Category-level references above; no paid customer for this offer was verified.

## Validation questions still unanswered

Who has a current, repeated problem and authority to buy? What do they use now, including free alternatives? Why would this offer be better enough to pay for? Which channel permits contacting them? What is the real acquisition cost and all-in support time? Which seller and identity details will buyers see? Which rights, data and professional rules constrain delivery?

Do not open an account or pay for tools merely to fill a category. Compare one cheap paid test with the existing CSV offer or another validated line. Keep private customer files and secrets out of this research tree.
