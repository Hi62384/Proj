## Commercial thesis

Focus on records, educational planning and licensed-data summaries. Validate with operators before building integrations or extrapolating precision from uncertain inputs.

**Principal obstacle:** No pesticide, veterinary, electrical safety or unsupported carbon-credit advice. Hardware dependence and seasonal demand complicate automation.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.

**Deliverable:** Record non-advisory purchases, batches, costs and owner-entered usage dates  
**Mechanism:** Self-service digital download · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 39–129 CAD.

**First test:** Get an operator-reviewed paid template test using fictional records
