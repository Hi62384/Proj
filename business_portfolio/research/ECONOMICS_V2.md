# Income quality, not headline budgets
Revision 2 · all scenarios are assumptions until measured

No exact offer has new verified paid demand. The purpose of the model is to reject unattractive work early, not to forecast guaranteed income. A listing budget is neither an agreed price nor a collected payment. A higher proposed quote is not evidence someone will accept it.

## One currency and all owner time
For a project, keep receipts and costs in one declared currency. Record an actual conversion only when needed and observed; the v2 CAD quote suggestions are not conversions of v1 USD ideas. Track proof, learning specific to the offer, acquisition including failed proposals, qualification, delivery, tests, communication, revisions, support and administration. Shared work is allocated once across projects. Do not treat AI execution time as the whole business workload.

Contribution before owner labor and tax = receipts − platform/processing fees − refunds/reserve − allocated tools/runtime/acquisition costs − fulfillment costs. Effective owner-hour contribution = that contribution ÷ **all** owner hours. Economic surplus at a chosen labor value = contribution − total hours × labor value. This is a management scenario, not a tax calculation or an accounting certification.

For a simplified model with fee fraction f, refund-reserve fraction r, cash costs C, hours H and owner-time target W:

`minimum customer quote = (C + H × W) / (1 − f − r)`

If fees are charged on tax-inclusive receipts or have different refund treatment, substitute the actual settlement rules rather than this approximation. CAD30/hour is an editable comparison assumption, not the user's chosen wage or a promised market rate. Zero sales leaves negative cash/time results; always record failed experiments.

## Why cheap service work can fail
A CAD300 project at an illustrative 20% fee and 5% refund reserve, with CAD25 acquisition cash, leaves CAD200 before labor/tax. At 5, 8 and 12 all-in owner-hours, that is CAD40, CAD25 and CAD16.67 per hour. At CAD30/hour, eight hours needs roughly CAD353.33 in quote under these assumptions. Reducing unpaid custom demos can help; removing QA cannot.

Upwork's actual fee is contract-specific within its published 0–15% range, and Connects are an additional acquisition expense when required. Fiverr's 80% seller share gives a useful 20% marketplace stress case. Do not apply one provider's rate to another or omit currency/tax/settlement effects. [Upwork fee](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee) · [Connects](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062898-Understanding-and-using-Connects) · [Fiverr](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/9234443621137-Your-earnings-page).

|---|---:|---:|
| CAD15 | CAD10.25 | CAD0.2050 |
| CAD9 | CAD5.03 | CAD0.1006 |

A 20% affiliate commission would reduce these numbers further; do not add affiliate costs on top without recalculating. At 0% conversion, ad value is zero. At 1%, the break-even click limits halve. These are not measured conversion rates or expected market click prices. A small ad budget cannot prove robust product-market fit.

`maximum sustainable acquisition cost per order = contribution before acquisition − required profit/owner-time allowance`

`maximum click cost = conservative paid-visitor purchase conversion × maximum acquisition cost per order`

Use observed paid orders, account for refunds/support, distinguish organic referrals from cold ad traffic and choose a conservative conversion assumption. Leads, checkout starts and views are not sales. For services, include lead-to-paid-client conversion and delivery capacity; cost per lead by itself is insufficient. Do not use speculative lifetime value to justify losing money on the first order.

## Commercial prioritization
Prefer a specific requested problem, existing customer infrastructure, low ambiguity, a reusable proof and a small acceptance scope. Reject geography/credential mismatches, unknown financial/regulated obligations, low-paid broad work, hidden production costs and false urgency. An attractive score is not a success probability. A profitable repeat order is stronger than an unaccepted proposal; repeated collected payments and measured support are needed before calling an offer low-touch.

Service plans include a favorable and stress calculation plus a quote-floor check. The lower price in many ranges fails the stress case; that is intentional evidence to narrow, reprice or decline, not an invitation to promise profitability. No monthly earnings target is generated by multiplying an unvalidated price by invented customer volume.
