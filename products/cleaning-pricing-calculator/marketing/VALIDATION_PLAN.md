# 7-day validation plan — distinguish product preparation from earning money

**Product hypothesis:** Independent residential cleaning operators will pay CAD $29 for an editable spreadsheet that makes jobs and cost structures more transparent. No sales or buyer interviews have been recorded for this exact product.

**Day 1:** Inspect three current comparable paid listings and two substantive free templates. Note exactly what the comparisons include, their stated prices, review dates, and any alternative that makes our offer unnecessary. Do not copy their workbook/formulas/design.

**Day 2:** With fictional data, have a real cleaning-business operator attempt a quote in five minutes. Ask where the worksheet fails, what is missing, and whether they already have a better method. Record their actions, not just compliments.

**Day 3:** Fix a demonstrated problem if it fits our scope. Independently test formulas and the blank/demo Excel files in native Excel. Review taxes, business identity and privacy before publication.

**Day 4:** Create the legitimate marketplace listing as a draft. Test checkout and automatic digital delivery under provider rules; record total per-sale fees and refund policy. Stop if the home address would be disclosed in an unacceptable way.

**Day 5:** Publish only with explicit approval. Prepare one useful pricing-example tutorial with fictional numbers and a link where permitted. No mass unsolicited messaging.

**Days 6–7:** Track impressions, qualified visits, sales, refunds and support time. Do not estimate earnings from hypothetical traffic. Record what buyers actually do.

**Decision at day 14:** A meaningful sample of qualified visits with 0 paid sales indicates possible price, value, channel or trust problems. Revise based on observed objections before creating variants. One sale is not proof of repeatability. Three independent paid sales are an early signal, not a statistical guarantee.

**Measurement:** Contribution per sale = CAD selling price before tax - Payhip fee - PayPal fee - refundable/dispute allowance - other variable costs - acquisition cost. Monthly operating profit subtracts fixed costs; economic profit additionally subtracts time used to create, market and support the product. Confirm settlement timing and tax obligations separately.
