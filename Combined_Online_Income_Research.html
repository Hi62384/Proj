The integrated decision

One initial offer, two credible routes to scale

A · Early paid validation

CAD $299 bounded reporting sprint

Respond to a buyer's explicit CSV/reporting request. Show the tested synthetic demo, agree acceptance, then obtain a funded milestone. After paid proof, standardize the delivery and test maintenance only when justified.

Strength: faster feedback from a paying buyer and higher ticket. Weakness: sales, implementation and support do not disappear.

B · Low-workload downloadable products

Original asset kit or local checker

The GitHub study recommends a 12-hour craft gate for five original assets, with a CAD $29 paid pack only if they meet commercial quality. Its second product is a CAD $49 offline localization QA checker.

Strength: repeatable digital fulfilment. Weakness: free alternatives, discovery, upfront production and uncertain conversion.

Recommended sequencing: test the paid reporting offer first if qualified buyers are reachable and the scope is deliverable. Use the asset craft gate as an alternate initial route, not a competing full workload. The offline QA checker, reporting toolkit and SaaS ideas follow evidence; do not build all three simultaneously.

Why sell services first?

The evidence can arrive in days: a buyer has a task, you demonstrate a bounded solution, and either receive a funded order or a meaningful objection. This is a hypothesis about access, not a first-week guarantee.

Why retain the original study?

It is more restrained about paid reach and fully accounts for delayed payouts and owner time. Its 24-month scenarios expose how low-ticket sales can fail to repay the build effort.

When automate?

After repeat paid usage. Automate reliable delivery and invoicing; keep safety, ambiguous metrics, privacy, customer commitments and production changes subject to approval.

Execution

First 72 hours

Day 1

Prove the delivery

Open the synthetic report demo and run the included tests. Define a CAD $299 pilot: one report from ≤2 authorized CSV exports, agreed metrics, verification, one revision and explicit exclusions.

No customer data before permission; no claim of prior customers.

Day 2

Find paid intent

Identify five truly relevant explicit reporting/automation requests or warm referrals. Send tailored responses where platform rules and applicable consent permit; request redacted sample rows and acceptance criteria.

Track sent, substantive replies, paid orders, time and objections.

Day 3

Obtain a funded commitment

Agree the scope and funded milestone. If paid, implement and reconcile the actual source. If not, diagnose problem/buyer/channel fit before building extra automation or dropping price.

A quote, reply or file download is not revenue.

Alternative: if paid service requests aren't accessible but asset production is already a demonstrated strength, test five original game assets under the repository's 12-hour craft ceiling. Avoid building a huge pack or two software products without validation.
