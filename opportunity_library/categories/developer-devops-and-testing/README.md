## Commercial thesis

Package a painful reproducible engineering task with fixtures, CLI outputs and regression tests. Sell a measurable saved step rather than a generic boilerplate dump.

**Principal obstacle:** Strong free alternatives and support across toolchains impose maintenance. Do not ship secrets or unsafe automatic production changes.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.
