## Suggested FAQ

**Does this calculate taxes automatically?** No. You must manually set the correct tax rate for your business and confirm tax obligations; the workbook has a default 0% placeholder, not an exemption opinion.

**Is there a recurring fee?** No. The included download uses ordinary Excel formulas and does not require a subscription from this seller. Microsoft Excel licensing, if needed, is separate.

**Will it connect to my bank, PayPal or booking system?** No. This is a standalone offline workbook and does not access financial accounts.

**Can I change the numbers?** Yes. Replace editable blue sample costs and service assumptions. Default values are fictional illustrations.

**Can I use this for customers?** You can use the workbook to prepare customer quotes for your own business after reviewing accuracy and compliance. The license is for internal use by one business, not resale or redistribution.

**Will you guarantee my profit?** No. The estimated contribution excludes unentered expenses and is not net profit or a promise about pricing outcomes.
