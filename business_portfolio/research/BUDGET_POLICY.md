# CAD100 ceiling, CAD0-first operating policy
Revision 2 · 2026-11-05 · planning, not spending authorization

## What the limit means
The user's “price for each project” is treated as **incremental owner cash required to validate/start it**, not the price charged to a customer. Because the owner is in Canada, the cap is expressed in CAD. Customer prices may be higher and are separately labeled hypotheses. No foreign-exchange conversion was used to present old USD asking prices as verified CAD prices.

Prefer CAD0. The **absolute ceiling is CAD100** per selected project through its first-sale/first-30-day validation experiment. Include tax, exchange/conversion charges, proposal credits, trial/API usage, ads, hosting and noncancelable commitments. A late first sale does not reset the cap; stop/review after 30 days. The launch ceiling is cumulative: a first sale or new month does not reset it. Further spending requires a separately revised owner-approved plan. The limit is not an instruction to spend the allowance. Current permission is **CAD0 across the entire portfolio**.

A separate project ID does not create another spending allowance. Shared proof, tools and acquisition work are charged once, with a consistent allocation. Parallel agents cannot each assume they may spend CAD100. An owner must approve an itemized commitment and remaining aggregate budget before any external purchase. This package does not connect to accounts and cannot technically block real-world charges; enforcement requires the existing permission boundary and actual billing controls.

## A free validation path, not a claim that everything is free
Use existing equipment, native free features and synthetic data. Reuse the current storefront and approved assets. No new domain, logo, email suite, database, paid AI API, self-hosting server or CRM is required for a proof. Existing equipment, electricity, sunk development and already-paid subscriptions are not valued at zero; they are simply not new purchases for this experiment. Owner hours are separately counted in the economics model.

A Make free tier has limited credits; an Apps Script account has quotas; a free ESP has a subscriber/send limit. A free trial or a connector that becomes paid after the trial is not a sustainable zero-cost stack. Platform-native automation may be preferable to adding an AI agent. [Make](https://www.make.com/pricing) · [Apps Script](https://developers.google.com/apps-script/guides/services/quotas) · [MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/help/free-plan-update-faq).

Customer production must use authorized customer-owned tools with current feature/volume checks. Record the required plan, included credits, expected peak usage, rate limits, data location, support and responsible account owner. A paid Shopify store is not “free” because Flow has no separate fee. Do not fund recurring client subscriptions or use the owner's laptop as production hosting. [Shopify Flow](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-flow).

If a required licence, secure hosting or expertise cannot fit the owner's cap, simplify the offer or defer. The six software projects remain **discovery-only**, not production-budget-approved. Never hide operating expense as “the client will pay” without the client's informed agreement and a price estimate.

## Suggested envelopes — not approvals
| Work type | Current spend | Optional future all-in allocation | Condition |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Scoped service | CAD0 | Up to CAD25 acquisition + CAD10 demonstration + CAD15 contingency = CAD50 | Qualified exact-scope opportunity and explicit approval; not every project needs any of these |
| Existing low-price download | CAD0 | A later maximum CAD25 ad test plus taxes/FX/other approved costs, all below CAD100 | Genuine paid orders, known contribution and measured conversion; not authorized now |
| Bundle/internal work | CAD0 | No independent budget | Allocate necessary labor/runtime once to parent |
| Deferred download/software | CAD0 | None at discovery stage | Paid need and viable production-cost plan required before reconsideration |

Transaction fees deducted from customer receipts are not an upfront purchase, but must reduce contribution. Reserve owner cash for any fees paid before settlement, refunds, taxes or other liabilities; if the arrangement could exceed the cash cap, decline or restructure it. Customer funding is not permission to ignore cash flow or legal obligations.

## Preflight and ledger
Before any proposed expense, record project ID, purpose, provider, actual quoted currency, current conversion/tax amount, upfront amount, committed renewals, whether the subscription auto-renews, refundability, customer-vs-owner payer, existing commitments and an approval reference. Missing cost is **unknown**, not zero. Decline auto-top-up and unattended renewals. Do not enter credentials or customer financial details into the public planning files.

A local synthetic budget check is included in `tools/commercial_checks.py`. It validates arithmetic and missing values; it cannot cancel a provider subscription, check a bank balance or confer approval. A private real ledger is needed for actual use. The included JSON template has no actual money or approval recorded.

## Advertising safeguards
A daily ad budget is not a hard experiment cap. Google states that most campaigns can bill up to twice the average daily amount on one day and 30.4 times that average in a month. Verify a supported total-budget configuration and account billing behavior, or choose a fixed-price placement with a firm approved limit—or do not run the test. Alerts and manual pauses are not guaranteed charge caps. Tax and FX remain inside CAD100. [Google Ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6385083?hl=en) · [budget types](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/campaigns/budgets/overview).
