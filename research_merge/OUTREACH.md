# Ethical first-sale outreach templates

Use these as manual, truthful drafts. They are not authorization to send unsolicited messages.
Start with explicit in-platform requests for matching work and permitted introductions.
Review applicable CASL/platform rules; a public email address is not blanket consent.
Keep a record of the lawful communication basis, identity details and any required opt-out.

## Reply to an explicit reporting-automation job
Hi [name] — your request mentions [specific repeated reporting task].
I am offering a fixed-scope CAD 299 pilot for one repeatable CSV-to-report workflow:
agreed field mapping, validation, source reconciliation and run instructions.

I have a local synthetic demonstration of the approach; it is not a prior-client case study.
Could you share 10 redacted rows and the exact output you need through this platform?
After checking the sample, I will confirm scope and a funded milestone before starting.
No migration or unrestricted account access is required for this pilot.

[Your truthful name/contact details appropriate to the platform]

## Permitted introduction request
I am testing a narrowly scoped reporting-automation service for small agencies that repeatedly
combine CSV exports. Do you know an operator who has explicitly asked for help with that task?
Please introduce us only with their permission. The first step is a small fixed-price pilot,
not a long subscription or a promise of guaranteed savings.

## Relevant follow-up when permitted
Following up on your request about [specific task]. The pilot would cover [bounded outcome] for CAD 299.
Please let me know whether the need is still active; no problem if you have already solved it.
[Identity/contact/opt-out details required for this channel.]

## Objection: “Our reporting software already does this”
That may already be the best solution. The service is only useful for an export, mapping or
validation gap your existing tool does not cover. There is no reason to purchase a duplicate system.

## Never do
Mass scraped outreach, automated LinkedIn DMs, purchased lead lists, false scarcity,
fake reviews/results, invented clients, unauthorized site testing, or off-platform fee avoidance.
