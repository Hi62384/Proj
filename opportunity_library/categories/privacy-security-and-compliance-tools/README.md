## Commercial thesis

Favor local inventory tools, evidence organization and permissioned read-only checks. Minimize what is collected and publish exact coverage instead of broad protection claims.

**Principal obstacle:** Never claim a tool guarantees security or legal compliance. Credentials, scan permission and professional responsibility make unsupervised operation inappropriate.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.

**Deliverable:** An original map of collected fields, retention, processors and deletion responsibilities  
**Mechanism:** Self-service digital download · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 39–149 CAD.

**First test:** Test with privacy professionals and sell a blank editable workbook
**Distribution path:** Permitted original-product marketplace, useful sample, opt-in audience or a specialist distribution partner.

**Human work remains:** Original product QA, licensing, customer discovery, accessibility, refunds and compatibility updates.
**Specific obstacle:** Not legal compliance advice
**Public privacy:** Minimize unnecessary public personal details, but determine exact seller, registry, licensing and customer disclosures before launch.
