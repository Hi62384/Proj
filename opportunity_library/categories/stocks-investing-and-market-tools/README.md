## Commercial thesis

Separate selling a useful research tool from investing your own capital. Research aids need licensed data and non-misleading outputs; capital returns are not startup-business sales.

**Principal obstacle:** No promised returns or automated client-money execution. Advice, trade signals, data redistribution and capital loss require a separate review and no automatic launch.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.
