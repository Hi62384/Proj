## Platform and cost constraints that change a quote

n8n’s pricing is execution-based; Make’s credits and Zapier’s tasks do not count work in an identical way. Polling, filters, branching, retries and AI-provider usage matter. A three-app workflow should not be sold as automatically free on Zapier’s two-step free tier. Make’s free credit allowance is not unlimited production capacity. Current exact prices/terms must be checked before a paid decision. [P01, P03–P05]

The October 2026 n8n licensing guidance is more nuanced than “all client work prohibited” or “self-hosting allows any SaaS.” It distinguishes customer-owned installations, internal staff editing with output delivery, and external access to configure workflows. Do not give clients editor/API/MCP/custom-interface configuration access or launch hosting/white-label services without checking the applicable commercial terms. This package defaults to customer-owned accounts to simplify ownership; it is not a legal license determination. [P02]

## The test that prevents invented differentiation

Ask: “What can the customer not achieve with the supported native feature, and why is resolving that gap worth more than all implementation and ongoing costs?” Record their answer and a concrete example. Faster configuration can be a legitimate service; it is not a proprietary software moat. Do not claim accuracy, uptime, savings or ROI without measured customer evidence.
