# Recommended experiments, not earnings rankings

Selection favors bounded deliverability, comparable buyer intent, low upfront cost and limited sensitive access. There is no validated win probability, average sale price or measured time to first customer. Rank differences are judgement, not statistically meaningful scores.

## What not to prioritize

Do not begin with a whole-business operating system, voice agent, cold-outreach engine, autonomous financial agent, multi-tenant credential service or generic template shop. Retrieved large budgets often bundle extensive scope and constraints; a bigger advertised number is not a better net-income opportunity. [J09, J12, J15, J16; P02, P06–P09]

Keep the other records available. The catalogue is broad on purpose, but budget, owner time and evidence should control what becomes active. Bundled components should not create independent revenue lines. A recurring service must earn repeat payments; it is not validated by a proposed subscription price.
