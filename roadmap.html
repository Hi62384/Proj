F Twelve-month execution roadmap

These are decision stages, not promised revenue dates. The model launches an asset in month 1, localization software in month 3, CSV pilots in months 6–8, and any gated subscription no earlier than month 9. Prototypes and interviews can happen earlier. Stop spending when evidence fails. A faster or different launch sequence requires a new model run.

Tools and capacity

Initial tools: existing art software, Godot, a local JavaScript runtime, source control and eligible marketplace delivery. Software phase: Papa Parse/Ajv or appropriate equivalents, fixture tests, Playwright where useful, checkout test mode and a local ledger. Hosted phase only after validation: commercial-compatible hosting, a queue/database and signed payment webhooks. Verify each dependency's licence, security and current maintenance status before adoption. See E and G for implementation details.

Reserve about 50% of initial time for product/testing, 30% for learning/acquisition and 20% for documentation, compliance and setup. Ring-fence support before growth. If maintenance exceeds six hours/week for four weeks, pause launches and simplify. If acquisition stays founder-led, call the business semi-automated. The model tracks all work separately and must override any generic time target.

Continue, expand or abandon

Continue learning when a repeated pain exists but the sample is too small. Expand when buyers pay, activate, return/refer, support is bounded and contribution is positive. Abandon or reposition after ten qualified demos plus two meaningful offer revisions produce no purchases, or when value depends on unwanted custom labour. Record evidence, time spent and the maximum cost of the next test.
