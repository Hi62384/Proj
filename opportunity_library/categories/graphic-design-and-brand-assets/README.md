## Commercial thesis

Sell original coherent systems with practical formats, accessible variants and licence clarity. A usable sample helps test whether buyers prefer the product to free assets.

**Principal obstacle:** Fonts, trademarks, stock restrictions and inconsistent AI acceptance matter. A reusable style system still needs art direction and QA.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.
