# Privacy review — must be completed before going live

The intended seller is an individual operating legitimately in Canada. The public-facing marketplace may expose information about that individual through seller profiles, payment statements, receipts, legal disclosures or buyer support, regardless of generic branding.

**Public privacy is not guaranteed by this product, Payhip, or PayPal.**

1. Confirm the legal seller name and the permissible business/trading name, especially if Ontario registration is required for use of a separate brand.
2. Inspect PayPal Business's actual customer statement description, customer-service phone, email, and business-address display. Do not enter fictitious information.
3. Preview Payhip's public seller profile, checkout, receipt, order email, invoice and refund path for a sample order using permitted sandbox/test processes.
4. Check whether the business/residential address is visible or disclosed by law. Don't take live payments until the owner accepts the disclosure or resolves it with a lawful verified alternative.
5. Avoid placing customer data, passwords, bank identifiers, home addresses or tax IDs in product files or a public repository.
6. Use a monitored real support email; don't claim a non-existent support phone, corporation, company address or customer results.
7. Enable multifactor authentication on your payment and seller accounts and use distinct passwords.
8. If a product later sells in a regulated market, re-check selling rules for the specific category instead of relying on the cleaning toolkit review.

The downloadable Excel workbook is entirely offline and contains no tracking, linked account credentials or merchant details. It should not be assumed to anonymize data users enter into their own copies.
