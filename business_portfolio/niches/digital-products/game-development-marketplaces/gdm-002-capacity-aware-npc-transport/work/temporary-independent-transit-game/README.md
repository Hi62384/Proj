# Temporary independent transit integration

Original synthetic test project with a game-owned character controller and two physical platforms. The test fixture is adapted from the product integration fixture and uses a local copy of the current candidate add-on. It is not an existing third-party game or evidence of general controller compatibility. Run Godot with `--headless --path <this folder> --script res://test_transfer.gd`.

`test_midride_restore.gd` saves the coordinator, passenger and first platform during travel, reconstructs those objects, resumes the physical ride and completes the transfer. The second platform and rest of the scene remain in memory. Both fixtures passed on standard Godot 4.4.1, 4.5.1 and 4.7.2 on Windows x86-64; root-certificate-store warnings were emitted. The fixtures are adapted from existing product tests and do not prove integration with an independently developed game, arbitrary world persistence, or general crowd navigation. The copied add-on files match the current review candidate by hash, excluding generated UID files.

`test_interruption.gd` disables a service while the passenger rides, checks the occupied seat remains held, moves the platform back to a known safe dock and waits for the game-owned controller to physically exit before acknowledging recovery and releasing the seat. It passed on the same three versions. The route and safe stop are scripted; this is not general vehicle replanning or emergency evacuation.

`test_onboard_cancellation.gd` uses the same physical safe-dock arrangement to cancel the passenger's journey while aboard. It verifies that the seat remains occupied until physical recovery and that the final journey state is cancelled. It passed on all three versions; this is one scripted cancellation path.

Run `python run_checks.py <godot-console-executable>` to execute the five headless cases with isolated user data. The runner passed 5/5 on 4.7.2. A separate `test_visual_transfer.gd` renders the same transfer with primitive meshes; hidden 4.7.2 OpenGL captures at 1152×648 were inspected for the passenger boarding, riding and reaching the destination. The first camera framing was too distant and was adjusted before the final capture. These are automated captures of a synthetic scene, not hands-on interaction, editor-gizmo acceptance or an existing game's visual integration.

`test_full_world_restore.gd` saves the coordinator, passenger and both platform positions mid-ride, destroys the test world, recreates both platforms and the fixed dock surfaces, restores the passenger and coordinator, and completes the trip. It passed individually on standard Godot 4.4.1, 4.5.1 and 4.7.2; the updated five-case runner passed 5/5 on 4.7.2. Fixed dock geometry is recreated from the fixture rather than serialized, and the route and timing remain scripted. This is not general game-world persistence.
