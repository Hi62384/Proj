Evidence limits

No independent customer interviews, our paid pilots, repeat payments or production workload tests were conducted. Each original row received a separate judgment using the linked evidence; this is not 96 separate market experiments.

Technical documentation establishes constraints, not demand. Vendor claims are not independent performance tests. Prices are asking prices, not sales. Reviews need not represent paid customers. Job posts are requests, not completed contracts. Some contract evidence is available only through indexed extracts with private earnings.

The original financial assumptions—including customer acquisition and support time—remain unvalidated. Do not infer profit probability from any classification or count in this review.

Before any substantial build

Require a genuine funded pilot for a problem not adequately solved by the customer's current tools. Seek three independent paid pilots and two actual repeat purchases before scaling recurring scope. These are proposed management gates, not statistical guarantees.

Measure acquisition, onboarding, processing, review, maintenance, support and administration—not merely the time needed to run a script. Stop or narrow the offer if alternatives are good enough, repeat demand is absent, or labor consumes the margin.
