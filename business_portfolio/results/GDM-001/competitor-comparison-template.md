# Historical-Save Test Workflow — Competitor and Substitute Comparison

**Use:** Blank research worksheet for GDM-001. No current market census, price check, product test, or competitor feature verification is claimed by this template.

## Research protocol

1. Choose a single engine edition and a specific buyer workflow. Do not compare unrelated engines as if they were interchangeable.
2. Check official product and policy pages first, then marketplace listings and dated release notes. Record the access date and exact version/plan where shown.
3. Separate vendor claims from hands-on observations and buyer reports. Do not infer quality, adoption, sales, or demand from a listing’s existence or review count alone.
4. Compare against the buyer’s current native tests and custom scripts, not only commercial products.
5. Use no account credentials, customer saves, private repositories, paid access, or outreach without separate authorization. Respect licenses and access limits.
6. Mark unknown fields as “not verified”; do not fill gaps by inference. Recheck before making a development, pricing, or distribution decision.
