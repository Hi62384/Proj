# Public privacy, AI use and distribution

## The achievable goal

Minimize unnecessary public personal information while using real, accurate details wherever identification, tax, banking, registration or contracts require them. A brand, logo, pen name, dedicated email or merchant of record can reduce personal marketing; none guarantees that customers or the public cannot identify the legal owner.

The library therefore stores **public-brand suitability**, **seller-specific disclosure review status**, and required private identity separately. Every record says `guaranteed_anonymity=false`. No identity verification has been completed by this project.

For Ontario, review business registration/search information before choosing a structure. For federal corporations, public disclosure of individuals with significant control can identify owners. Do not incorporate on the assumption that it creates anonymity. Applicable privacy obligations remain even when providers process customer data. [Ontario](SOURCES.md#r-ontario), [federal ownership disclosure](SOURCES.md#r-federal), [PIPEDA](SOURCES.md#r-pipeda).

## Prelaunch privacy inspection

Review the actual logged-out storefront, seller profile, developer/trader section, checkout, receipt, invoice, transaction descriptor, refund notice, terms and required registration entries. Record exactly which legal name, brand, location, address and email are visible. Test under the intended country, account type and platform mode. A general privacy statement is not a substitute for this check.

Use one dedicated business mailbox with appropriate aliases where supported, strong unique authentication, recovery controls and minimal integrations. Do not make duplicate marketplace identities to avoid limits. Strip unnecessary personal metadata from samples, file paths and Git commit metadata before publication; preserve provenance and required copyright/licensing notices. Keep customer data, identity documents, keys and actual financial statements out of this public research repository.

## One business, many products — conditional reuse

Share a brand/legal owner, support infrastructure, tested code, payment adapters and bookkeeping categories where provider rules and the product's risk permit. Separate brands may be useful; separate entities or registrations may be needed in particular circumstances. Shared ownership does not ring-fence liability, and extra platform accounts must follow each platform's rules. Never assume one paid licence covers resale, hosted embedding or every customer. [n8n licence](SOURCES.md#r-n8n_license).

## Finance and investing require a separate lane

Financial recordkeeping and factual source-linked research tools are not equivalent to making personal investment recommendations, executing client trades or promising returns. Review registration, advertising, data rights and the exact product scope before selling anything in these areas. CAPITAL records in the catalogue are excluded from business rankings and automated kit selection. [OSC](SOURCES.md#r-osc), [SEC data](SOURCES.md#r-sec), [market data](SOURCES.md#r-alphavantage).

## Marketing and customer data

Keep commercial outreach consent, identity and unsubscribe obligations separate from technical sending capability. A public email or an AI-generated proposal is not blanket permission to send. Use approved integrations, genuine purchase requests and permissioned introductions. Avoid misleading earnings claims, fake reviews, unauthorized voices/likenesses and low-value scaled search pages. [CASL](SOURCES.md#r-casl), [Google spam policies](SOURCES.md#r-google_spam).

The usual small-supplier GST/HST threshold is based on relevant taxable supplies and associated rules, not profit or a separate exemption for each new product. Confirm exact tax facts with a qualified professional; keep tax collected outside business revenue. [CRA](SOURCES.md#r-cra).
