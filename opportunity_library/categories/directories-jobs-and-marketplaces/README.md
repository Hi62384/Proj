## Commercial thesis

Start with a tightly defined information or matching utility and manual verification. Monetize only when one side receives demonstrated value.

**Principal obstacle:** Two-sided cold starts, stale entries, privacy and scams make a generic directory or job board slow to monetize; never fabricate listings or demand.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.

## Six additional concrete experiments

### CUR-24-01 · Opt-in regional supplier capabilities directory
**Buyer:** Small purchasing teams  
**Deliverable:** Verified voluntary profiles with capability fields, dates and correction routes  
**Mechanism:** Licensed information product · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 19–99 CAD.

**First test:** Get ten consenting suppliers and one paying information user first
**Distribution path:** Source-linked sample for a specific decision maker; paid trial and partner distribution.
**What can be automated:** Licensed ingestion, normalization, provenance, change comparison, packaging and subscriber delivery.
**Human work remains:** Rights review, factual verification, missed-change checks, editorial judgement and renewal usefulness.
**Specific obstacle:** Stale listings and two-sided demand
**Public privacy:** A branded product can avoid face-led promotion. Checkout, invoices, contract documents, registries and mandatory disclosures still need review.
**Advance only when:** Redistribution rights confirmed and paid repeat demand demonstrated; document coverage and omissions.
**Stop or revise:** Do not sell when source rights, freshness, false negatives or analyst workload are unresolved.
**Evidence:** Category-level references above; no paid customer for this offer was verified.

## Validation questions still unanswered
