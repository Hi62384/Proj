# Product spec and quality boundaries

- Product: Cleaning Business Pricing & Profit Calculator, v1.0
- Format: Modern .xlsx with no macros, no links, no automated cloud uploads
- Target buyer: Small residential cleaning firm with basic Excel competency
- Price to test: CAD 29, unvalidated
- Customer license: perpetual internal use for ONE business, no redistribution
- Compatibility claimed: `.xlsx` intended for modern Microsoft Excel; native Excel must be smoke-tested by the seller
- Supported currency: CAD, no conversion; user must review sales taxes
- Quote math: loaded labor, paid travel, mileage, consumables, fixed/additional overhead, contingency, target contribution margin, payment fee allowance, discount, tax, rounded list pricing
- Features: independent quick quote, view-only draft quote, up to 25 what-if job scenarios, up to 25 actual-job log entries, KPI overview, seven service rows
- Doesn't include: tax/CPA advice, payroll processing, scheduling, CRM, invoice sending, automatic transaction sync, refunds, marketplace traffic, payment integration, profit guarantees
- Data: Fictional examples; no seller/person identity, bank details or real customer results in product
- Change discipline: after editing formulas, rerun independent tests and manually check in Excel, then rebuild the customer ZIP and update version/manifest
