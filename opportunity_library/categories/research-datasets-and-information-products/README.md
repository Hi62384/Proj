## Commercial thesis

Sell a meaningful filter, quality check, normalized export or original analysis with provenance. Prefer lawful bulk/API sources over uncontrolled scraping.

**Principal obstacle:** Open access is not unlimited redistribution. False negatives, stale data and ongoing analyst time can be expensive.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.
