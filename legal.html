Canadian legal and practical checks

This is general planning guidance, not individualized legal, tax or accounting advice. The user's province, registration status, other income, customer locations and contracts are unknown. Obtain a Canadian accountant or lawyer for the areas below when material. The public sources were checked for this report, but launch-time verification is still necessary.

Business and tax

  • Check provincial/territorial business-name registration, municipal licensing and whether operating under a trade name requires registration. A sole proprietorship may be a simple starting structure; incorporation is not assumed to save tax or be necessary.
  • Report business income and eligible expenses using the appropriate Canadian filing route; for a sole proprietor this generally includes T2125. Report revenue even below the GST/HST threshold. Keep receipts, gross sales, refunds, platform fees and currency conversions. CRA generally requires business records for at least six years. S71 S72
  • The usual CAD30,000 GST/HST small-supplier threshold is based on worldwide taxable supplies across the relevant businesses/associates, not profit or each storefront separately. Crossing it in one quarter differs from crossing it across consecutive quarters. Zero-rated exports and marketplace/MoR contracts complicate the calculation; obtain advice before approaching the threshold. S70
  • A merchant of record's transaction-tax handling does not eliminate Canadian income tax, CPP, bookkeeping, provincial tax analysis or every registration duty. Review who legally sells to the customer and what the creator supplies to the MoR.
  • Use an illustrative tax reserve, not a claimed tax rate. Other income, deductions, province, entity type and CPP can make actual liability materially different. Foreign withholding and tax-interview forms must be verified; do not assume a treaty automatically yields zero withholding.
  • Keep transaction tax outside revenue when it is collected for authorities. Check whether tax on platform fees is recoverable. Treat registration/professional advice costs as launch contingencies rather than hiding them in “free.”

Consent and privacy

Commercial email and similar electronic messages generally need an applicable consent basis, sender identification and an unsubscribe method under CASL. A public email address is not universal permission, and a request for consent can itself be a commercial message. Prefer opt-in research participants, permitted public community posts and useful inbound resources. Keep the source/date/scope of consent and process opt-outs. A purchase receipt, product-service update, newsletter and abandoned-cart campaign have different purposes; do not enable every marketing automation by default. S68 S69

Collect only what a product needs, publish an accurate privacy notice, protect access, specify retention, support applicable access/deletion requests and maintain a breach-response process. PIPEDA, provincial rules and foreign customer laws can apply. Local-only file processing substantially reduces transmission but analytics, checkout and support can still process personal information. The initial business tools should avoid children's, health, payment-card and other sensitive data. Obtain specific permission and an appropriate contractual/security basis before processing customer-owned private files or using an AI provider. S73

Intellectual property and truthful offers

All asset packs, audio, code, text and samples must be original or appropriately licensed for the exact redistribution. A licence to use an asset in a game often does not allow resale of the standalone file. Open source can require notices, source distribution or other obligations. AI output rights, training provenance, marketplace acceptance and copyright protection are distinct issues. Keep a manifest of authors, sources, licences and changes.

Do not commercialize Coraline characters, names, art, music, distinctive assets or other third-party fan-mod material without verified rights. A free fan project does not create permission to sell a derivative product. General development techniques can inform a new original product, but the commercial package needs its own clean rights chain.

Disclose affiliate and sponsored relationships clearly. Claims about time saved, accuracy, security or income must be supported. Do not create fake testimonials, purchases, reviews or engagement; do not describe AI output as human-verified unless it actually was. S74

Platform and contract gates

Before choosing a platform, check Canada payout eligibility, identity/tax verification, prohibited categories, refund rights, payout holds, reserve policy, currency/FX, commission changes, AI disclosure, licence terms and any exclusivity. A supported country does not guarantee account approval. Do not count seller approval as complete until it is verified. Consumer cancellation/refund rights in buyer jurisdictions can override a seller's preferred “no refunds” wording.

Before the first sale, prepare plain-language product scope, compatibility, licence, refund/cancellation and support terms. Explain recurring charges and limits before checkout. Do not promise legal compliance, perfect translations, all tenders, guaranteed deliverability, guaranteed income or zero downtime. Publish nothing and accept no new agreement as part of this research task.
