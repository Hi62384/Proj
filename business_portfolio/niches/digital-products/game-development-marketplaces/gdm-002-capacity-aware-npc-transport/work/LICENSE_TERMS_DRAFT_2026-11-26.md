# Proposed GDM-002 usage terms — local draft

**Status: draft for review. This is not an operative license, a grant of rights, or authorization to distribute.** The owner selected commercial game use and modification, with no standalone redistribution, on 2026-11-26. Ownership and the exact final files still need to be confirmed.

## Intended terms for review

- A person or organization that obtains a valid license may use and modify the licensed add-on and included examples to develop commercial and non-commercial games.
- The licensee may distribute the add-on, including modifications, as incorporated into a game they distribute. Distribution as part of a game is intended to remain permitted even where the game format makes scripts technically extractable.
- The licensee may not redistribute, sell, sublicense or make available the add-on or its modifications as a standalone asset, library, template or competing package.
- These proposed terms cover only material that the eventual licensor has authority to license. They do not grant rights to Godot, third-party material or other unrelated assets.

## Decisions and review still needed

- Identify the actual rights holder and verify provenance and redistribution authority for every file in the final delivery.
- Define access for team members and contractors, whether there are seat or project limits, transferability, and what happens to released games if a license ends. Do not assume these terms have been selected.
- Decide how source-available games, public repositories, game mods and development builds should be handled without undermining the standalone-redistribution restriction.
- Decide applicable warranty, liability, consumer rights and governing-law language, and ensure the final terms and marketplace conditions are reviewed for the intended jurisdictions. Support duration, contact and response expectations are not yet established.
- Review and approve final text and the exact delivery before distribution. The current local review archive remains unlicensed and unpublished.

A restriction against standalone redistribution is not an OSI-approved open-source license merely because source is included. See the Open Source Initiative's Open Source Definition, checked 2026-11-26: https://opensource.org/osd . No OSI approval is claimed.
