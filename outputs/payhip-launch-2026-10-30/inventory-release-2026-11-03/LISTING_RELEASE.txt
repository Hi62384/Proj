Inventory Reorder Planner

Review a stock reorder threshold and the gap between that threshold and the units you have available. A focused, editable spreadsheet for one item or scenario at a time.

FOUR INPUTS, TWO OUTPUTS
Enter daily unit demand, supplier lead time in days, safety stock and units available. Reorder point = demand x lead time + safety stock. Gap to threshold = MAX(0, reorder point - units available). This gap is not an optimized order quantity.

The fictional example uses 12 units/day, 7 days, 30 safety-stock units and 80 units available. It produces a threshold of 114 and a gap of 34. Replace these illustrative inputs with your own assumptions.

INPUT RULES AND LIMITATIONS
Enter all four nonnegative numbers. Zero is valid; missing, text or negative inputs display CHECK INPUTS. Decimals are allowed and quantities are not rounded to whole units automatically. Check pack sizes and supplier minimums yourself.

This is a single-item planning aid. It does not maintain a stock ledger, forecast demand, track purchase orders or stock already on order, account for supplier reliability, or place orders. Use separate copies to compare scenarios.
