## Commercial thesis

Read-only preflight, reconciliation and specialized merchant utilities can be tested without editing a live store. Partner with agencies after repeated merchant demand.

**Principal obstacle:** Existing inexpensive apps cover generic tasks. Production catalog writes, returns and private customer data require strong controls.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.

## Six additional concrete experiments

### CUR-22-01 · Supplier CSV cleanup and preflight
**Buyer:** Small merchants  
**Deliverable:** Normalize one approved supplier file and create a dry-run import error report  
**Mechanism:** Fixed-scope service · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 399–799 CAD.

**First test:** Sell an export-only pilot after reviewing a redacted sample
**Distribution path:** Relevant explicit purchase requests, permissioned introductions, or a small partner pilot.
**What can be automated:** Intake validation, deterministic transformations, test fixtures, packaging and draft delivery notes.
**Human work remains:** Qualifying buyers, scope agreement, ambiguous data, output acceptance, confidentiality and disputes.
**Specific obstacle:** Overwriting live catalogs and existing import apps
**Public privacy:** A brand can reduce personal marketing. Contracts and marketplaces such as Upwork can disclose a verified name to customers.
**Advance only when:** Two independent accepted paid jobs, bounded scope and measured contribution after sales/delivery time before expanding.
**Stop or revise:** Pause expansion when custom exceptions exceed the quote or focused buyer conversations reveal no willingness to pay.
**Evidence:** Category-level references above; no paid customer for this offer was verified.

**Related workflows, not independent market proof:** FAST-O02, REPO-O04.

## Validation questions still unanswered

Who has a current, repeated problem and authority to buy? What do they use now, including free alternatives? Why would this offer be better enough to pay for? Which channel permits contacting them? What is the real acquisition cost and all-in support time? Which seller and identity details will buyers see? Which rights, data and professional rules constrain delivery?

Do not open an account or pay for tools merely to fill a category. Compare one cheap paid test with the existing CSV offer or another validated line. Keep private customer files and secrets out of this research tree.
