## Commercial thesis

Original evergreen tutorials and reusable editing assets can support branded publishing. Compare a licensed asset product with an editing service before committing to an audience-only model.

**Principal obstacle:** Ad monetization has audience gates; repetitive mass production, reused footage and unapproved likenesses undermine eligibility and privacy.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.

## Six additional concrete experiments

### CUR-02-01 · Original tool-explainer video channel
**Buyer:** Viewers learning a specific practical workflow  
**Deliverable:** Faceless screen-recorded lessons with original demonstrations and downloadable examples  
**Mechanism:** Audience, content or affiliate · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 0 CAD.

**First test:** Publish three genuinely useful episodes and measure qualified product clicks before scaling
**Distribution path:** Useful original content, permitted search/community distribution and disclosed affiliate or sponsor relationships.
**What can be automated:** Editorial assistance, original-source checks, scheduling via approved tools and broken-link monitoring.
**Human work remains:** Fact-checking, editorial originality, audience acquisition, sponsor disclosure and continual refresh.
**Specific obstacle:** Audience dependency and production labor
**Public privacy:** A branded product can avoid face-led promotion. Checkout, invoices, contract documents, registries and mandatory disclosures still need review.
**Advance only when:** A reachable audience and independently measured monetization; check native eligibility before production scale.
**Stop or revise:** Stop mass output when quality, originality, terms or unit economics fail; no purchased fake engagement.
**Evidence:** Category-level references above; no paid customer for this offer was verified.

### CUR-02-02 · Authorized podcast highlight edits
**Buyer:** Independent video podcast teams  
**Deliverable:** Six manually reviewed short clips from one buyer-owned recording with captions and source timestamps  
**Mechanism:** Fixed-scope service · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 200–500 CAD.

**First test:** Offer one fixed-length episode pilot with two example edits and one revision
**Distribution path:** Relevant explicit purchase requests, permissioned introductions, or a small partner pilot.
**What can be automated:** Intake validation, deterministic transformations, test fixtures, packaging and draft delivery notes.
**Human work remains:** Qualifying buyers, scope agreement, ambiguous data, output acceptance, confidentiality and disputes.
**Specific obstacle:** Poor clip selection and repetitive revisions
**Public privacy:** A brand can reduce personal marketing. Contracts and marketplaces such as Upwork can disclose a verified name to customers.
**Advance only when:** Two independent accepted paid jobs, bounded scope and measured contribution after sales/delivery time before expanding.
**Stop or revise:** Pause expansion when custom exceptions exceed the quote or focused buyer conversations reveal no willingness to pay.
**Evidence:** Category-level references above; no paid customer for this offer was verified.
