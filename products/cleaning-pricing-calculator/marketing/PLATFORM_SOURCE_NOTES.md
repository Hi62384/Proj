# Payhip platform source notes — reviewed 2026-10-14

Official information, checked for this product's draft listing. These facts establish platform capabilities, not approval for this seller, tax treatment, product demand, or privacy guarantees.

- [Payhip pricing](https://payhip.com/pricing): Free Forever plan is **$0 per month plus a 5% platform transaction fee**; PayPal/Stripe processing fees still apply separately. This is not a promise that total fees equal 5%.
- [How to add a digital product](https://help.payhip.com/article/59-adding-a-digital-product): Products → Add new product → Digital Product; upload the ZIP; add title, price, cover and description. File size limit listed as 5GB. Visibility choices include Visible, Unlisted and Invisible. Digital goods are delivered automatically on payment and via purchase confirmation email.
- [Connect PayPal](https://help.payhip.com/article/64-connecting-your-paypal-account): Account → Settings → Payment Details → connect PayPal, for supported sellers including Canada, with provider-specific verification. The provider states both personal and business PayPal accounts can be connected from supported countries; check the user's specific account.
- [Refund requests](https://help.payhip.com/article/281-refund-request): Sellers manage refund policy and responses, subject to applicable legal obligations. Do not promise 'no refunds' categorically.
- [Test checkout](https://help.payhip.com/article/59-adding-a-digital-product): Payhip describes using a 100% off coupon for a test order. Those test orders appear in Customers and are not deletable. Check seller privacy before using a real PayPal payment method.
- [Storefront structure](https://payhip.com/blog/meet-your-new-payhip-store/): Payhip has stated each account supports one public storefront; different public brands may require separate accounts. This does not prohibit multiple products under one eligible storefront, subject to Payhip's current rules.

**No seller account was connected, no test purchase was made, and no listing was published in this project.** Recheck platform terms, pricing, supported methods, customer display fields, billing data and taxes during actual setup.
