# Further research: selection, acquisition and budget fit
Reviewed 2026-11-05 · 41 additional source records · no customer contact or paid validation

## Decision
The most defensible first test is still a small implementation or diagnostic for a buyer with an existing system and a specific current problem. Prefer fixing a missing mapping, diagnosing one failed webhook, or delivering one form-to-CRM path over a vague “AI agent”, content factory or multi-tenant SaaS. This is an editorial conclusion from buyer-request specificity, tool competition and cost/effort constraints—not a measured probability of success.

The priority is not to build more before selling. Qualify a real request, reuse one small proof, quote a bounded result and measure the all-in economics. Keep multiple options available, but run at most one primary and one related fallback acquisition experiment at a time. Owner time can be expensive even when cash spending is zero.

## What changed in the evidence
**Agency implementation is real stated demand, but not an assured opportunity.** A newly reviewed indexed marketing-agency request asks for customer-owned n8n/Make setups, cost estimates, checking and handover (V2-29). Another operations request separates assessment from implementation (V2-32). These support narrow delivery and paid diagnostics; neither establishes this owner's win rate or earnings.

**Low headline budgets are often traps for this goal.** Retrieved examples include US$100 for AI lead qualification, US$50 for broad social-content automation and US$200 across four US$50 guides (V2-30/V2-31/V2-40). Broad fixed-price commitments can fail the workload model. The revision does not copy those budgets into profitable forecasts.

**Higher asking budgets are not automatically accessible.** One tracking listing is US-only (V2-37). A MailerLite job requires established expert marketplace credentials (V2-38). A strategic automation request shows substantial competition and experience expectations (V2-39). These are filtered out unless eligibility and competence are actually met. Job status, applicant counts and advertised prices are snapshots, not confirmed current vacancies, contract values or payment records.

**Existing-stack document work is worth a bounded test.** A Sheets/Docs reporting request advertises US$500 for a broader setup (V2-33). AWA-122 narrows this to one approved template, deterministic substitutions and manual review, with no sensitive assessments or reporting-model redesign. This is an adjacent demand signal, not evidence someone has bought that exact smaller offer.

**Technical funnel setup is not “ensure it sells.”** A Payhip/email/author-site request supports adjacent demand for setup help (V2-34), but includes more work than checkout QA. New AWA-123 and AWA-124 explicitly sell a checked delivery path or consent-based native sequence—not guaranteed traffic, deliverability or revenue. Tracking AWA-125 stays skill-gated and does not imply an analytics event proves incremental sales.

## Free/native competition and costs
Make has a limited free-credit allowance; Apps Script has quotas; MailerLite's current free plan is limited to 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails per month (V2-06/V2-12/V2-14). A required premium feature or provider usage charge cannot be ignored to advertise a CAD0 setup. Customer-owned platforms are the default production path, after checking exact feature, volume and cost requirements.

Use native Shopify Flow when adequate instead of adding a new automation subscription; an existing eligible paid Shopify plan is still required and some actions are plan-gated (V2-15). Use the existing CRM connector, ESP automation, project template or vendor report where it solves the problem. The value is a well-scoped correct outcome and supportable handover, not merely using AI.

The old n8n licence URL did not resolve during this review. The current repository documentation was used instead (V2-11); the former URL's failure is recorded rather than silently treating it as read. Consulting and unrestricted hosted-product rights are not interchangeable. Keep production licence review as a gate.

## Selling the existing downloads
Payhip's free subscription still has transaction and payment-processing costs (V2-01). Its marketplace has a genuine-sale eligibility threshold and review (V2-02); do not assume it supplies the first customer. Free Jobber cleaning templates and Smartsheet inventory templates are real alternatives (V2-24/V2-25). The revised existing-product records include honest positioning, reusable demonstration assets, permitted organic distribution, funnel metrics and stop rules.

Low-price downloadable products have little room for paid acquisition when support and fees are included. The shared model shows the break-even arithmetic using explicitly hypothetical processor/refund/support inputs, rather than claiming current ad prices or conversion. Ads are in the launch plans as a conditional later experiment, not an automatic expense.
