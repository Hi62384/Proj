## Commercial thesis

Sell administrative workbooks, verified information or customer-owned content resources. Begin with one business workflow and an honest time-saved comparison.

**Principal obstacle:** Food safety, allergens, real-time travel data and venue policies are not safe to guess. Repeated local updates reduce passivity.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.
