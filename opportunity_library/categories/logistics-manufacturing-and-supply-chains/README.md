## Commercial thesis

Use bounded exports and exceptions to solve unit mismatches, fulfillment delays or inventory visibility. Read-only pilots avoid operational disruption.

**Principal obstacle:** Bad quantities, units or lead times can halt operations. Avoid automated machine control and safety-critical guidance without qualified oversight.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.
