Cedargrid Digital — Support and Refunds

For purchase support, use our store’s Contact page or the seller contact on your Payhip receipt. Include your order number, purchase email and a brief explanation. For workbook issues, include your spreadsheet application and version. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or confidential customer records.

We aim to respond within two business days, excluding weekends and Ontario public holidays. Support covers downloading, opening and using the supplied files as described. Custom development, bookkeeping and tax or business advice are not included.

If the product does not meet your needs, request a refund within 14 calendar days of purchase, even if you have downloaded it. We also review duplicate charges, missing delivery, corrupted files and material differences from the description. You may choose a corrected file instead of a refund.

Approved refunds go through the original payment provider to the original payment method where supported. Processing times depend on the provider.

Nothing in this policy limits rights or remedies provided by applicable law, which may extend beyond our voluntary refund window.
