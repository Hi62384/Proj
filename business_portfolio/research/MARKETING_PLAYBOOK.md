# Marketing is part of the project—not an afterthought
Revision 2 · draft activities only · CAD0-first

## The acquisition sequence
Select one primary offer and at most one related fallback test, not 56 simultaneous launches. Keep the broad portfolio for later choice. A small work-in-progress limit prevents paying for many unproven funnels while preserving the user's larger portfolio goal.

1. Identify an actual buyer and buying event. Record the current workaround, costly failure, native/free alternative and acceptance owner.
2. Define one result and an explicit exclusion list. Use a 60–90 minute reusable fictional proof, not free custom production work. Do not claim experience or results the owner does not have.
3. Prepare a compact sales asset: one outcome headline, relevant screenshot/test, scope, customer tool requirements, limitations, test-price hypothesis and one call to action. An existing profile, allowed community post or existing Payhip listing is enough; no new website is required.
4. Reach the buyer through a permission-appropriate channel. Use the project-specific discovery phrase and channel, not a generic “we automate everything” campaign.
5. Ask for a redacted example and scope agreement; use a funded bounded milestone or approved arrangement before client-specific implementation.
6. Measure all time/costs, collected payments, acceptance, refunds and support. Use real customer permission for a testimonial or case study, then assess repeatable delivery.

These are sequential gates, not a guarantee of sales. The first unpaid-work limit is deliberately small. When evidence is weak, pausing saves time; writing more code does not establish demand.

## Service channels and scripts
**Primary no-cash discovery:** manually review explicit hiring requests in the official Make Hire Help area or n8n Jobs, separating buyers from sellers and respecting category rules. Platform membership is not partner accreditation. Current availability, eligibility, price, security and scope must be checked for each request. [Make hiring](https://community.make.com/c/hire-a-pro/hire-help/74) · [Make posting rules](https://community.make.com/t/looking-to-hire-an-expert-or-offer-your-services-hire-a-pro-is-the-right-space/96837) · [n8n jobs](https://community.n8n.io/c/jobs/13).

**Secondary:** a genuine warm referral or one permitted public demonstration. There is no assumed existing audience or referral network. If neither an eligible request nor permission-appropriate channel exists, the free route has not been established; pause rather than label customer acquisition solved.

**Optional paid acquisition:** a few individually selected Upwork proposals only after checking Connects and actual contract fees, geography, credentials and available cash. Never scrape/apply automatically or create fake work history. No bidding credits are purchased by this request. A new Fiverr profile is not a default infrastructure task and has no guaranteed discovery.

Each service record includes a project-specific draft response. Use the buyer's actual problem and a relevant acceptance test, not a mass-generated opener. A suitable message asks for the missing scoped information and does not claim a fictional sample is past client work. Follow marketplace communication/payment rules.

Use two batches of at most four eligible requests, with a maximum four total unpaid owner-hours or fourteen elapsed days. Four silent attempts are a reason to inspect targeting/proof, not a statistically valid declaration of no demand. Spend the second batch only after a useful adjustment; at the cap, pause and record the result. A buyer conversation, signed scope, funded milestone and collected money are distinct stages.

## Digital products: the complete small launch
Use EX-001 and EX-002's specific copy/asset plans. Before a new download is sold, its project must contain: buyer problem, native/free substitutes, supported environment, honest worked example, finished deliverable inventory, licence/refund/support terms, accepted payment method, a tested permitted delivery path, distribution route and a measurement plan. Do not list an unfinished product as immediately deliverable.

A useful free sample should demonstrate a specific paid benefit; it is not evidence of willingness to pay. Existing low-price products already have releases: reuse those and do not restart authoring or storefront setup. The first test is one or two permission-appropriate placements with source tracking, not a daily-content job for weeks.

Product copy should answer “why this instead of the free alternative?” through an observable difference. Do not invent a bundle discount, claimed original price, bestseller status, review count, guaranteed savings or limited stock. Paid templates face free/native/AI competition; honesty about compatibility can reduce conversions but is still necessary.

## Advertising included, but gated
Advertising is not automatically efficient. Current ads allocation is CAD0. Paid traffic can be considered only after genuine sales and measured contribution show room for acquisition cost. Three independent paid orders and a measured funnel are a minimal management checkpoint for considering a tiny learning test, not statistical proof of profitable scaling. Do not infer cold-traffic conversion from friends/referrals or manufacture purchases to pass a gate.

For a later approved test, use **one offer, one audience and one conversion action**, with a written hypothesis and an all-in maximum CAD25 initial spend. Prefer exact problem intent over broad “AI” interests. Draft two honest headlines from the project's outcome and one proof-based description. Examples: “Fix missing CRM form fields” / “One field map. A checked result.”; for downloads, reuse the existing product headline, not a claim of guaranteed profit. Research keyword availability and eligibility before any ad purchase; this package does not estimate current click prices.

Record the expected audience, destination, exact claim, approved creative, conversion action, source tag, attribution caveats, spend ceiling, time boundary and stop condition. Reject the test if required minimum budgets, tracking, lawful consent or billing controls do not fit. Do not install pixels or upload customer lists without separate permission. Google average daily budgets are not hard total caps; verify current controls or do not launch. [Budget rules](BUDGET_POLICY.md).

Stop immediately on a broken destination, wrong price, tracking failure, misleading claim, consent issue or capped spend. Do not automatically increase budget after clicks or one sale. Continue only when the small test produces a credible signal worth a separately approved follow-up; CAD25 is not enough to claim broad statistical certainty. No spend/creative optimization runs unattended here.

## Email, affiliates, SEO and repeat business
Use genuine opt-in email only, with appropriate sender identity and unsubscribe behavior. Social DMs can be commercial electronic messages; “B2B” or a publicly displayed address does not automatically authorize outreach. Consult the CRTC guidance and get appropriate advice for uncertainty. Do not use a consent-request email as a loophole to blast strangers. [CRTC FAQ](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm).

Affiliates may be a later revenue-share channel, not free guaranteed distribution. Require an actual willing partner, disclosed connection, a commission that fits contribution, accurate claims, clear payout/refund handling and permission. Payhip's seller-managed payouts add work. No affiliate is recruited or paid by this plan. [Payhip affiliate help](https://help.payhip.com/article/95-affiliates) · [Competition Bureau](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act).

SEO and content can compound but are not assumed fast first income. Write one useful answer/example for a verified recurring question; avoid mass AI pages, a new blog infrastructure project or an unmeasured publication treadmill. Existing satisfied customers are a sensible place to ask about a genuinely recurring problem; offer a maintenance scope only after that need exists. A subscription is not passive without measured exception/support labor.

## Metrics and interpretation
Track eligible requests → approved responses/placements → qualified visits/replies → scoped conversations/checkout starts → funded/paid orders → accepted delivery → refunds/repeat orders. Deduplicate channels and exclude synthetic/free/self test orders from genuine demand. Use aggregate/pseudonymous public notes; keep actual contacts, orders, financial evidence and consent records private. The JSON experiment template contains null outcomes, not fabricated data.
