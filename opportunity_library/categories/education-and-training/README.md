## Commercial thesis

Prefer an original, assessable learning outcome with worked solutions and a small paid test. License to organizations when the material truly solves a recurring training need.

**Principal obstacle:** Teaching expertise and accuracy cannot be outsourced blindly. No copyrighted test banks, academic cheating services or unsupported certification claims.

These are screened concepts, not verified profitable businesses. Current category/platform sources establish alternatives and constraints, not willingness to pay for our exact products. Test-price ranges, effort and scores are unmeasured planning assumptions.

### CUR-13-01 · Practical spreadsheet audit mini-course

**Deliverable:** Short original lessons with tested workbooks, exercises and worked answers  
**Mechanism:** Self-paced education · **Unvalidated asking-price range:** 39–149 CAD.

**First test:** Sell one clearly scoped module before filming a full course
**Distribution path:** Focused paid pilot lesson, relevant instructors or organization licences before a large course catalogue.
**What can be automated:** Delivery, practice checks, source-controlled examples, enrolment and routine progress reporting.
**Human work remains:** Teaching accuracy, accessibility, student questions, updates and truthful credentials.
**Specific obstacle:** Teaching quality and learner acquisition
**Public privacy:** A branded product can avoid face-led promotion. Checkout, invoices, contract documents, registries and mandatory disclosures still need review.
**Advance only when:** Paying learners demonstrate the intended learning outcome and support demand fits the price.
